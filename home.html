Skeptic Loves It

After wandering around the house thinking I was being made a fool of for 10-15 minutes, I found my Fitbit One. Sure, I had to watch a few adverts, but it was worth it. The only complaint (and the reason I had to watch ads to be granted more searches) is that during discovery mode, my wife's Fitbit HR and my One kept changing sort order on the screen so fast that I accidentally clicked hers the first few times. Also, when searching, the app occasionally hung and turned gray. Not sure, but minimizing and reselecting the still-open app seemed to force it to continue. Have a little patience, move slowly, and you'll find your device so long as it's still transmitting!

- JesseSkeet

Search nearby bluetooth devices.

Browse a list of bluetooth devices near you. Walk around while checking on the device name and signal strength(Numbers on the right side of the screen) to identify the one you're looking for.

Filter your device list

Filter your device list by singal strength so that devices that are close to you will always appear on top. You can also filter the list by device name so it's eariler to read, becuase the order of the list will not change too often in this mode.

Locate your device,
in minutes.

To focus on the device you're interested, simply tap on the device on the list to open up this search mode. Walk around while checking on the singal strength and distance to locate your device.
